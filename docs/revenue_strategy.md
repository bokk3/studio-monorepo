# Monetary Optimization Scheme & Revenue Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary
We are building a unified ecosystem of three distinct titles:
1. **Web F2P** (Accessible, browser-based entry point)
2. **Mobile F2P** (On-the-go engagement, high retention)
3. **Console/Steam Premium** (AAA experience, high fidelity, upfront purchase)

All three games share a unified backend powered by **Supabase Authentication**, allowing a single **Player Profile** to track progression, inventory, and currency across the entire ecosystem. This document outlines the monetization strategy, the cross-game economy, and the specific mechanics used to drive revenue and engagement.

## 2. The Unified Cross-Game Economy
The core philosophy is **"Play Anywhere, Progress Everywhere."** The single Player Profile ensures that time and money spent in one game inherently benefit the player in the other two.

### 2.1 Unified Currencies
- **Gold (Soft Currency):** Earned through gameplay across all three platforms. Used for basic upgrades, low-tier cosmetics, and entry fees for standard events.
- **Diamonds (Hard Currency):** Purchased with real-world money. Used for premium cosmetics, battle passes, high-tier lootboxes, and convenience items. Diamonds are universally accessible across Web, Mobile, and Console.

### 2.2 Cross-Pollination of Player Base
- **Funneling:** Web and Mobile F2P titles act as massive top-of-funnel acquisition channels. As players become invested in the universe, they are incentivized to purchase the Console/Steam Premium title.
- **Retention:** Players who buy the Premium title can continue their engagement on Mobile during commutes or on Web during breaks, increasing total lifetime value (LTV).

## 3. Free-to-Play Monetization Model (Web & Mobile)
The Web and Mobile games rely on a robust Free-to-Play (F2P) model designed to maximize engagement and recurrent spending.

### 3.1 Gacha & Progression Mechanics
- **Lootboxes & Chests:** Players can earn standard chests through gameplay or purchase premium lootboxes with Diamonds. These contain a mix of cosmetic items, account boosts, and crafting materials.
- **Card Packs:** A collectible system where players open card packs to unlock new characters, abilities, or modifiers. High-rarity cards are positioned as chase items.
- **Skins & Cosmetics:** A vast array of character skins, weapon wraps, and profile customizations. Available via direct purchase with Diamonds or through randomized drops.

### 3.2 Engagement & Habit-Building Loops
- **Login Bonuses:** Daily escalating rewards for consecutive logins. Day 7 and Day 30 milestones offer premium currency (Diamonds) or exclusive card packs to drive habituation.
- **Daily Drops & Quests:** Time-gated daily and weekly quests that reward Gold and standard Chests. Encourages players to return every day to complete their routine.
- **Battle Pass (Cross-Platform):** A seasonal progression track offering free and premium tiers. Purchasing the Premium Battle Pass with Diamonds unlocks high-value skins and currency multipliers.

## 4. Premium AAA Integration (Console / Steam)
The Console/Steam game is a premium title ($49.99 - $69.99 upfront). However, it deeply integrates with the F2P ecosystem to create added value.

### 4.1 Value Proposition for F2P Players
- **The "Premium Status" Upgrade:** Purchasing the Console/Steam game grants the player's unified account a permanent "Premium Status." This status provides passive bonuses in the Web and Mobile games, such as a 10% boost to Gold earned and slightly increased luck in standard lootboxes.
- **Exclusive Unlocks:** Buying the Premium game immediately unlocks an exclusive set of skins, characters, and a hefty sum of Diamonds that can be used across all platforms.

### 4.2 Cross-Progression Rewards
- **Achievement Sync:** Unlocking high-tier achievements in the AAA game grants unique cosmetic titles and banners that are proudly displayed on the player's profile in the Web and Mobile games.
- **Resource Generation:** Specific activities completed in the Console game can generate daily "care packages" that the player can open the next day on their Mobile or Web app, cementing the multi-device loop.

## 5. Conclusion
By leveraging Supabase for a seamless unified backend, we create an ecosystem where F2P accessibility drives player acquisition, aggressive but fair monetization on Web/Mobile drives recurrent revenue, and the Premium AAA game serves as the ultimate anchor for our most dedicated fans.
